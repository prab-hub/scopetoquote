How It Works

Three steps. Done in minutes.

No setup fees. No implementation. You'll have your first estimate in the time it takes to make a coffee.

01
📄

Drop in your scope

Paste a project description or upload whatever the client sent — PDF, Word doc, forwarded email, Notion page. ScopeToQuote extracts the deliverables and maps them to line items automatically.

02
🤖

AI finds your closest past work

Your estimate history trains a personal matching engine. For every new scope, the AI surfaces the most similar previous estimates — with a match score — so you're always building on real data, not gut feel.

03
✅

Review, adjust, send

Import the matched line items with one click. Adjust quantities and prices as needed. Apply tax, payment terms, and your branding — then export a professional PDF that looks like you spent an hour on it.
